AI Workflows

Recruiting, with AI.

The workflows I’ve built to keep recruiting organized, make everyday tasks easier and create more space for meaningful conversations.

01

One Stop Hiring Hub

I built a self-serve Hiring Hub for the three businesses I support — a form where a hiring manager picks their group, title, level, and location, and it generates a first-draft job description, panel prompt, interview guide, scorecard and market insights report on the spot, pulling from our existing SharePoint templates where they exist.

02

Boolean search builder

Uses job descriptions and intake notes to create Boolean search strings with broader options to explore the talent pool and targeted searches to refine results.

03

Status reporting

Turns pipeline data and notes into clear updates for leadership in an email friendly format.

04

Dashboards

Bring pipeline, hiring data and daily priorities into one view, helping me track each search, draft follow-up messages and plan a balanced workload.

05

Skills Repo

A collection of the AI skills I've built for recruiting, organized in one place on GitHub.
