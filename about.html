About

a little about me.

I bring over a decade of recruiting experience to my role as a Senior GTM Talent Partner at Adobe, where I support Strategy Consulting, Strategic Sales and Partner Sales. My experience also includes hiring across People Analytics, HR, Sales Operations, AI Strategy and Marketing.

Previously, I spent five years in agency recruiting, specializing in finance, accounting and business systems roles. I began my career in project management, producing audiobooks for Random House and planning large medical conferences. Those experiences shaped my approach to recruiting: coordinating stakeholders, managing complex timelines and keeping each search moving forward.

I approach AI workflows with a practical mindset, starting with repetitive tasks and building tools that make recruiting more organized and efficient. My goal is to create more time for meaningful conversations, while keeping human judgment and the candidate experience at the center of the process.

LinkedIn Recruiter, Workday, Phenom, Salesforce CRM, BrightHire, HackerRank, AI-assisted workflows
